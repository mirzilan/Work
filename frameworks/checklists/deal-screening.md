# Checklist: Deal Screening & Go/No-Go Decision

Quick filter before committing time to full IC paper. Use this to screen inbound opportunities, qualify pipeline, and surface red flags early.

**Time to complete:** 30–60 minutes (with access to term sheet, basic financials, CapIQ/Bloomberg).

---

## Deal Basics

| | Check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Asset type** | ☐ | Within our target investment universe? (sector, geography, stage) |
| **2. Size** | ☐ | Within our check size? ($[X]M–$[X]M equity check typical) |
| **3. Stage** | ☐ | Greenfield/acquisition/operating/distressed — do we have bandwidth? |
| **4. Sponsor** | ☐ | Known developer/operator? Credit quality? Prior relationship? |
| **5. Timeline** | ☐ | Realistic? Close in [X] months? FID by [date]? |

**Go/No-Go:** ☐ Proceed | ☐ Pass

---

## Market & Fundamentals

| | Check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **6. Sector tailwind** | ☐ | Market growth, policy support, or structural demand? |
| **7. Competitive position** | ☐ | Defensible? Unique offtake, location, tech, or contract terms? |
| **8. Peer multiples** | ☐ | Comparable companies trade at [X]x EBITDA / [Y]x Revenue? (check `market_intelligence/benchmarks/`) |
| **9. Macro sensitivity** | ☐ | Interest rate / FX / commodity moves material? Hedged? |
| **10. Regulatory risk** | ☐ | Permits, grid connection, environmental approval path clear? Timing risk? |

**Go/No-Go:** ☐ Proceed | ☐ Pass

## Financial & Structuring

| | Check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **11. Revenue model** | ☐ | Predictable? (contracted, off-take agreement, regulatory tariff, or merchant exposure?) |
| **12. Capex/ build cost** | ☐ | Well-defined? EPC contract in place or credible budget? |
| **13. Opex** | ☐ | Reasonable vs. peers? O&M contract, staffing, spare parts budget? |
| **14. Working capital** | ☐ | Any significant tie-up? Seasonal patterns? |
| **15. Tax & depreciation** | ☐ | Tax holidays, loss carryforwards, or incentives? Useful life assumptions realistic? |
| **16. Debt capacity** | ☐ | Banks willing to lend? Leverage ratios (Debt/EBITDA, Debt/RAV) acceptable? |
| **17. Return profile** | ☐ | Expected IRR [X]–[Y]% range reasonable for risk/stage? MOIC [Z]x acceptable? |
| **18. Cash flow timing** | ☐ | Distributions realistic? Any draw-down or refinance risk? |

**Go/No-Go:** ☐ Proceed | ☐ Pass

---

## Deal Structure & Documentation

| | Check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **19. Deal mechanics** | ☐ | Equity / debt mix? Pari-passu or subordinated? Preferred return? |
| **20. Control & governance** | ☐ | Board seat? Veto rights on capex, financing, sale? Drag/tag provisions? |
| **21. Offtake / offtaker** | ☐ | Long-term contract? Counterparty investment-grade? Price escalators, volume guarantees? |
| **22. Construction/ commissioning** | ☐ | EPC performance guarantees? Liquidated damages? Sponsor undertaking? |
| **23. O&M contract** | ☐ | Long-term O&M in place or credible sponsor? KPIs, performance guarantees? |
| **24. Exit path** | ☐ | IPO, strategic sale, dividend recapture, or refinance plan realistic? Timeline [X]–[Y] years? |
| **25. Key man / sponsor risk** | ☐ | Concentration with 1–2 people? Succession plan? Insurance? |

**Go/No-Go:** ☐ Proceed | ☐ Pass

---

## Red Flags & Deal-Breakers

| | Flag | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Offtaker downgrade risk** | Counterparty in distress or showing weakness? | STOP |
| **B. Uncontracted / merchant revenue** | Significant price or volume risk without long-term hedge? | STOP / Caution |
| **C. Execution risk** | Unproven technology, first-of-a-kind, or sponsor with weak track record? | Caution |
| **D. Regulatory uncertainty** | Major permits still in permitting queue? Policy risk? | Caution |
| **E. Sponsor equity** | Insufficient skin in the game? (typical: 20–40% of capex) | Caution |
| **F. Debt refinance cliff** | Balloon maturity, refinance risk, or unfavorable amortization? | Caution |
| **G. Commodity/FX hedging** | Material unhedged exposure to volatile inputs? | Caution |
| **H. Related-party transactions** | Sponsor selling inputs, O&M, or real estate at above-market rates? | Caution |

**Any STOP flags?** ☐ Yes → **PASS** | ☐ No → Continue
